Zombie Squad: Survival War – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026 · Developer: SauAI Labs · Contact: help@sauai.in

Summary

Zombie Squad: Survival War stores your progress (level, coins, gems, heroes, guns, settings) on your device. If you sign in with Google Play Games, a copy of that progress is also saved to your Google account so you can continue on another phone. We do not run our own servers and we do not collect your email, contacts, location or photos.

Information processed by third parties

The game uses these Google services. They collect data under their own policies:

  • Google AdMob (rewarded and full-screen ads). AdMob may collect the device advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data, to show and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where you consent, personalise ads. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), the game shows Google's consent form before any ad. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • Google Play Games Services (optional sign-in and cloud save). When you sign in, Google shares your Play Games player ID and display name with the game, and your saved progress is stored in your Google account (Saved Games). You can sign out or delete your Play Games data in the Google Play Games app or your Google Account settings. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases). Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. We receive only a purchase confirmation for the item you bought; we never see your payment details. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases and ads

The game offers optional in-app purchases with real money and optional reward videos. Random rewards (hero recruitment and the lucky wheel) show their odds in the game. You can turn off purchases in the Google Play Store settings (Authentication → require authentication for purchases), and "Remove Ads" removes forced full-screen ads.

Children

The game is not directed at children. It is intended for players aged 16 and over because of realistic violence against zombies and in-app purchases.

Your choices

  • Delete all game data: Settings → Reset progress, or uninstall the app. To also remove the cloud copy, delete the game's data in the Google Play Games app (Settings → Delete Play Games account and data, or per game).
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Change ad consent: reinstall the app, or contact us and we will explain how to reset it.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
