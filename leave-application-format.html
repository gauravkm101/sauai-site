Leave Application Format

Office, school and college — ready wording you can copy

A leave application is read in ten seconds by someone who has twenty other things to do. It needs four things and nothing else: who you are, how long you will be away, why, and who is covering. Everything beyond that makes it slower to approve.

Sick leave — office

To, The Manager, [Department Name] [Company Name] Subject: Application for sick leave Respected Sir/Madam, I am unwell and unable to attend office. I request you to grant me leave for [number] days, from [start date] to [end date]. I have informed [colleague's name], who will handle my pending work during this period. I will remain reachable on phone for anything urgent. Thank you. Yours sincerely, [Your Name] [Employee ID] · [Department] [Date]

Casual leave — office

To, The Manager, [Department Name] [Company Name] Subject: Application for casual leave Respected Sir/Madam, I need to attend to [a personal matter / a family function] and request casual leave for [number] day(s), from [start date] to [end date]. My current work is up to date and [colleague's name] has agreed to cover anything that comes up. I would be grateful for your approval. Yours sincerely, [Your Name] [Employee ID] · [Department] [Date]

Sick leave — school (written by a parent)

To, The Class Teacher, [Class & Section] [School Name] Subject: Leave application for my [son/daughter] Respected Ma'am/Sir, My [son/daughter] [student's name] of Class [class & section] is suffering from [fever / stomach ache / viral infection] and is unable to attend school. Kindly grant [him/her] leave for [number] days, from [start date] to [end date]. [He/She] will complete the missed work on returning. Thank you. Yours faithfully, [Parent's Name] Parent of [Student's Name] [Date] · [Phone Number]

बीमारी की छुट्टी — हिंदी में (कार्यालय)

सेवा में, प्रबंधक महोदय, [विभाग का नाम] [कंपनी का नाम] विषय: बीमारी के कारण अवकाश हेतु प्रार्थना पत्र महोदय/महोदया, सविनय निवेदन है कि मेरी तबीयत ठीक न होने के कारण मैं कार्यालय आने में असमर्थ हूँ। कृपया मुझे [तारीख] से [तारीख] तक [संख्या] दिन का अवकाश प्रदान करने की कृपा करें। मेरा कार्य [सहकर्मी का नाम] देख लेंगे। किसी आवश्यक कार्य हेतु मैं फ़ोन पर उपलब्ध रहूँगा/रहूँगी। धन्यवाद। भवदीय, [आपका नाम] [कर्मचारी संख्या] · [विभाग] [दिनांक]

Emergency or same-day leave

When something has already happened and you are informing rather than requesting, say so plainly in the first line. Do not open with an apology — open with the fact.

Subject: Urgent leave — [today's date] Respected Sir/Madam, Due to a family emergency I have had to leave immediately and will not be able to attend office today, [date]. [Colleague's name] is aware of my pending items. I will update you by [time / tomorrow morning]. [Your Name] · [Employee ID]

Getting it approved faster

  • Say the exact dates. "From 12 to 14 March" is approved faster than "for a few days".
  • Name who is covering. This is the single line that turns a request into an easy yes.
  • Do not over-explain the reason. One line. Medical detail is not required and makes the note awkward to forward.
  • Send it before the leave, not during. Where that is not possible, the emergency format above is the honest way to write it.
  • Do not attach a certificate unless asked. Most companies only need one beyond three days.
