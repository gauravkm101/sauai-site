Delete your SauAI account

App: SauAI (in.sauai.app) · Developer: SauAI Labs

You can permanently delete your SauAI account and associated data at any time. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Option 1 — In the app (instant)

Open SauAI → Me tab → Delete account → confirm.
This permanently deletes your profile, sign-in link, creations, credit balance and history, published posts, and generated media.

Option 2 — By email

Email help@sauai.in from the email address linked to your SauAI account with the subject "Delete my account". We will verify ownership and complete deletion within 7 days, then confirm by reply.

What is deleted

  • Account & sign-in data (name, email, photo)
  • All creations, prompts and generated images
  • Credit balance, coins, streaks, rewards history
  • Published posts, likes, comments, follows

What may be retained

Purchase records required for tax/legal compliance are retained in anonymized form as required by law. Google Play retains its own purchase records under Google's policies.
